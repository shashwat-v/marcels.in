Our Story

"We never wanted to be a café.
We wanted to be yours."

There's a particular kind of morning that only happens in a neighbourhood you know by heart — the one where you walk in, nod at the face behind the bar, and your cup arrives before you've found your seat.

Marcel's was built for that morning. Not for the city, not for the algorithm — for the block. Every detail, from the coffee to the corners, was chosen so this place feels like it was always here.

We serve espresso the way it deserves to be: unhurried, honest, and worth sitting down for. Come once and you'll see. Come twice, and you're already a regular.

—Rudraks, Founder
